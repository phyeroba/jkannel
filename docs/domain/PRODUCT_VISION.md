# JKANNEL Product Vision

## Vision

JKANNEL will become the leading open management platform for enterprise
SMS infrastructure.

The platform will simplify deployment, configuration, monitoring and
operation of SMS gateways while exposing the full capabilities of the
underlying engine.

## Vision Statement

Provide network operators, enterprises, ISPs and SMS aggregators with a
modern operations platform that replaces manual configuration with
intuitive management, automation, visibility and APIs.

## Long-Term Objectives

-   Modern web management
-   API-first ecosystem
-   Multi-engine support
-   Enterprise observability
-   High availability
-   Multi-tenant readiness
-   Extensible plugin architecture
-   Commercial-grade reliability

## Product Position

JKANNEL is not an SMS gateway.

JKANNEL is the management and operations platform around SMS gateways.
