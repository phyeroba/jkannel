# JKANNEL Logging & Audit Engineering Specification

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# 1. Purpose

The Logging and Audit subsystem records everything that occurs within JKANNEL.

It provides operational visibility, forensic evidence, troubleshooting capability, compliance reporting and business intelligence.

Every significant event generated anywhere within JKANNEL shall produce either:

• Operational Log

• Audit Record

• Metric

or

• Alert

The Logging System is separate from the Audit System.

Operational logs explain **what happened**.

Audit records explain **who performed the action**.

---

# 2. Objectives

The subsystem shall:

Provide centralized logging.

Provide structured logging.

Support live log streaming.

Support historical searching.

Support forensic investigations.

Support compliance audits.

Support security investigations.

Support troubleshooting.

Provide log retention.

Support log export.

Provide immutable audit history.

---

# 3. Logging Philosophy

Everything produces logs.

Everything important produces audit records.

Nothing important disappears.

No log shall require SSH access to read.

No engineer should have to tail Docker logs during normal operations.

JKANNEL becomes the single operational console.

# 20. Performance

Logging shall never block production traffic.

Asynchronous logging is preferred.

Batch writes where practical.

Log failures shall not crash the application.

# 22. Acceptance Criteria

The Logging & Audit subsystem is complete when:

- Every module generates structured logs.
- Every administrative action generates an audit record.
- Correlation IDs function end-to-end.
- Live log streaming works.
- Log searching works.
- Sensitive information is redacted.
- Retention policies function.
- Exports function.
- Compliance reporting is supported.
- Audit history is immutable.
